What this reproduces, and what it doesn't

From the paper

Han et al. (2022) combine Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-3 optical imagery in a deep network (UMRCGM). Sentinel-3's biophysical processor derives LAI and CCC from optical reflectance; the network then learns to predict those same values from Sentinel-1 SAR alone, so the field can be monitored even when cloud or fog blocks the optical sensor — common through Bihar's foggy Rabi winters.
